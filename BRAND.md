## Surfaces

- **Light mode**: page background `[hex]`, panel and card color `[hex]`

Panels are rounded with no outline. Bright colors are too light for small text, so darken them for headings and body text on light backgrounds and lighten them on dark ones.

## Avoid

- Borders on one edge of a panel, and thin lines across the top of the page
- Heavy use of gradient fills
- Anything that makes the UI look generic or AI-made
